This year, I started reading Douglas Adam's masterpiece, The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. There is no question about how funny, witty, and smart the story is. The whole thing is hilarious, and I absolutely recommend it for some amazing comedy content. But I can help but wonder: is there more to it? Is this book just trying to be funny, or is it also trying to say something? I am not sure, but let's try and find out.
