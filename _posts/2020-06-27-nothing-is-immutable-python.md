I love challenges. This summer, I came across an opportunity to try and make impossible things happen, as part of a software project. I said: bring it on. Now, what I was trying to achieve is certainly possible in theory. In practice, however, humans are limited: our brain can only hold so much in its working memory. This is why we need abstractions as a way to cheat our brains in order to manage to build complex systems. It works, but there are certainly drawbacks.

Abstractions require walls. And walls are not so good.
