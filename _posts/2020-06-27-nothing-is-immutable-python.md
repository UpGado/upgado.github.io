I love challenges. This summer, I came across an opportunity to try and make impossible things happen, as part of a software project. I said bring it on. What I was trying to achieve is certainly possible in theory. In practice, however, it was very hard to implement. The reason being that humans are limited; our brain can only hold so much in its working memory. This is why we need abstractions as a way to cheat our brains in order to manage to build complex systems. Abstractions work, and they certainly have helped us humans make great progress. However, they also have unavoidable drawbacks.
