Truth is, everybody has their preferences when it comes to the food they eat. Yet, we (at least broke college students) agree that the food you eat daily shouldn't break the bank. Well.. If you eat nothing, you do a great job saving but not so-great one at staying alive. You also have to get the nutrients you need in the optimal proportions (aka you can't rely on just one nutrient as the source of all calories).  It's an optimization problem that you, like it or not, have engage with everytime you choose what to eat. If so, why not optimize?

"Okay, but how do you do that?" is the question I will try to answer here in an accessible, enjoyable way! You can always use a pen and paper but that is boring. What if you can write a program, have fun while doing so, and then just plug-and-chug? Here I will explain how I approached the problem discussed, touching on concepts in economics and mathematics. For the sake of the nontechies, I won't include code here, but it is available at the end if you want to take a look.
