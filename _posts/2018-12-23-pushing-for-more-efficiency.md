Note: this is a dump of my mind, a conversation with my exteremely-ambitious inner-self. Nevertheless, I encourage you to engage with these thoughts and give them a thought after reading.

Start:

I am in love with living more, and I am not talking about prolonging life. I am talking about **packing more life in one's life**

YES HELL YES is the answer to questions of the form "I kinda wanna do X, should I do it now?"

No matter how long I (and probably you) live, life is too short to try everything that we could enjoy. It is too short to grow in all the ways we could grow.

Think about the wildest dream of who you could be. How much do you need to grow in order to get there? I hope the answer is *a LOT of growth*, because otherwise, you are not being ambitious enough.

How do we live more?
- [Try Everything!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6rP-YP4c5I) What is something that you've always wanted to try but "never" had the chance to? Fine. Maybe the time did really never come. But you shouldn't forget about it. Have it in the back of your mind. Put it in a Google Doc. The poit is: constantly remind yourself that there are those things that you want to try!
- Dare to try what you are bad at. Personally speaking, I know that I am gifted in my ability to learn so many computer things, and I am quite proud of that. However, there are *so many* things I have never got the chance (or guts) to try, including: art, music, and lots of "humanities subjects". It is time to grow.
- Apply [Lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lean_manufacturing) to your life. Lean means to maximize things that adds value, and eliminate things that doesn't. To know what adds value to your life, you have to know your values. Know what you want in your life. What is important to you and makes you happy? Make that 100% of your time. What things do you do that you are indifferent about? What do you do that actually makes you sad? Eliminate. Rinse and repeat, and you got yourself a life that you are passionate about.
