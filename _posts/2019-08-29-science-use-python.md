For the past 2 years now, I have been working at an academic research lab at Tufts University. Since my lab specializes in microscopic biomedical imaging, we generate *many* images, which in turn are analyzed to conclude some medically-relevant information. Of course, data analysis of microscopic images is kinda impossible to do by hand, so it's all programmed, which is yay. What is not so yay, however, is that the code used for this analysis is, for unfortunate reasons, terrible. Moreover, I would like to argue that this problem does not just affect my lab, it affects *all* scientific researchers that I came across, either in real-life, or online. Why is that?
