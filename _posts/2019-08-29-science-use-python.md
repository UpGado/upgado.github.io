For the past 2 years now, I have been working at an academic research lab at Tufts University. Since my lab specializes in microscopic biomedical imaging, we generate *many* images, which in turn are analyzed to conclude some medically-relevant information. Of course, data analysis of microscopic images is kinda impossible to do by hand, so it's all programmed, which is yay. What is not so yay, however, is that the code used for this analysis is very badly written. This problem does not just affect my lab, it affects *all* scientific researchers that I came across, either in real-life, or online.

Who cares? you might, fairly, ask. After all, the focus of scientific research should be the *actual science*, not the kind of data analysis that they carry out. In other words, the data analysis is just a means to an end; as long as you correctly analyze the data, does it matter what pile of code you have accumulated to achieve that?

The answer is a bold **YES**
